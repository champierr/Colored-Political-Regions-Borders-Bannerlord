Bug fix for **Colored Political Regions & Borders** on **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord**.

- **Fixed:** black squares along the borders after a battle, or after walking around a town, village or arena. Before such a scene the game frees the campaign map's graphics memory, and the texture the mod draws the border glow with did not come back. The mod now builds its map again when you return. Thanks to the Nexus user who reported it!
- **Tested:** if your clan is not in a kingdom, changing its banner colour recolours your land within a second.

Saves are not affected: just replace the old `ColoredPoliticalRegionsBorders` folder (or let Steam update the Workshop item).

**Tested and stable:** Bannerlord v1.4.8 (Steam), Sandbox campaign. **Not tested yet:** other game versions, the War Sails DLC and other mods – feedback welcome.

Also on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3829655051) and [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/14368).

### Downloads
- `ColoredPoliticalRegionsBorders_Bannerlord_v1.0.1.zip` – the module. Copy the `ColoredPoliticalRegionsBorders` folder into the game's `Modules` folder (replace the old one); `HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt` (EN/TR) is inside.
- `cprb_bannerlord_install.mp4` – 1.5 minute install video (EN/TR captions).
