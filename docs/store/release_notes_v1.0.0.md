First release of **Colored Political Regions & Borders** for **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord**: the political map of the [Warband version](https://github.com/champierr/Colored-Political-Regions-Borders), rebuilt for Bannerlord.

![cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/champierr/Colored-Political-Regions-Borders-Bannerlord/main/docs/images/cover.jpg)

- Zoomed out: every kingdom in its own colour. Zoomed in: normal terrain with dark borders and a glow of each side's colour.
- The map follows every conquest within a few seconds; new kingdoms, rebel clans and your own clan get their colours too.
- **Ctrl+1** on the campaign map: colour strength 0-100 %, saved for every game.
- Follows day and night; sea and lakes stay untouched; saved games are not changed; no other mod needed.

**Tested and stable:** Bannerlord v1.4.8 (Steam), Sandbox campaign. **Not tested yet:** other game versions, the War Sails DLC and other mods – feedback welcome.

Also on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3829655051) (subscribe and tick it in the launcher) and [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/14368).

### Downloads
- `ColoredPoliticalRegionsBorders_Bannerlord_v1.0.0.zip` – the module. Copy the `ColoredPoliticalRegionsBorders` folder into the game's `Modules` folder; `HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt` (EN/TR) is inside.
- `cprb_bannerlord_install.mp4` – 1.5 minute install video (EN/TR captions).
