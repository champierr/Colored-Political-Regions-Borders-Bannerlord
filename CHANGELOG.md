# Changelog

## v1.0.1 (2026-10-30)
- Fixed: black squares along the borders after a battle, or after walking around a town, village or arena. Before such a scene the game frees the campaign map's graphics memory, and the texture the mod draws the border glow with did not come back. The mod now builds its map again when you return.
- Tested: if your clan is not in a kingdom, changing its banner colour recolours your land within a second.

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-29)
- First release, tested on Bannerlord v1.4.8 (Steam), Sandbox campaign.
